Projects

My Development Showcase

I love building new tools and learning new technologies. Here are a few projects I'm proud of.

coala.io

One of my earliest and most well-known projects, coala brought together over a thousand developers to make coding easier and more consistent. It's a testament to the power of community in open source.

SameRun

Built in just two weekends to learn Flutter and Firebase, SameRun allows people to run together remotely. A fun project born out of necessity during the pandemic.

Hofly

A complete e-commerce marketplace for farmers, built single-handedly in about 3-4 months. Hofly has the potential to be my most impactful project, promoting local and sustainable food habits.
